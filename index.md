## Why ElfHosted instead of self-hosting or another host?

Convenience, idk anyone else who offers such great bundles and limits.

I don't have to selfhost either which is just annoying to set up and maintain

**How much time did your old setup take each month?**

Not much, like a few minutes, it was the rate limits that were annoying

**What did you use before ElfHosted?**

Midnight's instance I think

## Is ElfHosted what I expected?

I expected what was advertised, I already knew going in what it was and it's been the exact same as I expected

## What can you run on ElfHosted?

I run the AIOmagic bundle to use on stremio, I use TorBox, EasyNews and Real Debrid as providers, I use Elfhosted's great tool Debridge which has been great for Usenet content.

For add-ons inside I use torrentio, the elfhosted add-ons, all those sorts of things and it's been working flawlessly for a while, I've been with Elfhosted for about 3/4 months overall and never once have I had to put in any complaints or requests for bugfixes

Apps I chose to name: aiostreams, [postersplus](https://docs.elfhosted.com/app/postersplus/), [aiometadata](https://docs.elfhosted.com/app/aiometadata/).

I watch on: Fire TV, Phone, Tablet, Computer.

The apps I watch with: Stremio.

ElfHosted customer since 2026.

## What works well?

The thing I use most is the AIOmagic bundle, it's fast, a fair price, setup is easy and if it isn't then you can pay for staff to do it for you which is a great accessibility tool for those that need it, though I didn't.

**What do you no longer have to do since moving to ElfHosted?**

I no longer need to think about self-hosting, maintenance, fixing what breaks, I know all that is handled by the team while I just add or remove add-ons from AIOstreams.

Actually, on the team they've always gotten fixes out fast so that's another great thing about the reliability the elfhosted team oroivdes

**How often do you use AIOStreams?**

In stremio? Daily, my whole family loves it for all our content needs.

Tweaking? Every week or two I'll look and see if new add-ons have been added to the marketplace

## What goes wrong, and how does it get fixed?

Nothings gone wrong so far.

However, the elfhosted store is really slow, I have gigabit ethernet and it still takes a few seconds to switch between pages when on other sites it's instant

**Did you, Sam or the ElfHosted team sort it out?**

No, it's not really a complaint I'd send to the team as I'm certain they already know about it or have decided it's not a direct issue that needs instant sorting, also, visiting the store isn't a common thing anyway since once you're on your dashboard is where you're likely to spend your time and that's nice and fast

## Has it changed my free time or family life?

It's made our streaming much easier

**How many devices or screens does your ElfHosted stack serve?**

2 TVS, 2 iPads, 3 Phones, 2 Computers, 2 laptops

## How useful is Sam, ElfHosted's AI assistant?

I've used Sam to change some of the code stuff in AIOstreams to activate jellystreams, other than that though I'd say Sam is a neat idea that needs more work, it's just not a particularly useful tool half the time

**Was there anything Sam could not help with?**

Diagnosing issues in AIOstreams

**Did Sam get it right the first time?**

No, nor did it get it right at all, I solved it myself

## What is ElfHosted support like?

On discord I asked about credentials for the AIOposters bundle and they clarified my question in like two minutes or so

**How long until it was fixed?**

It wasn't anything that needed fixed and the whole interaction took two minutes from a ticket request to completion

**Did you ask in a public channel or in a private ticket?**

Private ticket

## Is ElfHosted worth the money?

Absolutely, it's the convenience of it, I can either pay for a bunch of apps... Or, I can get the AIOmagic, put it in stremio and boom, everything is condensed into one great and functional app

**Which subscriptions, hardware or hours did ElfHosted replace for you?**

Netflix, Disney+, HBO, Sky, Amazon Prime video

**Which plan or apps do you pay for?**

AIOmagic

I'd suggest it to most people with a smidge of technical knowhow, I don't it would suit old Dorris down the street who hasn't touched a smartphone in her life and doesn't know what an email is
